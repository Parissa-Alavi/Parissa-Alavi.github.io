About

Mechanics, materials, and code in the same workflow

I am a mechanical and materials engineer with a PhD from EPFL. My research has focused on building predictive computational models for nonlinear, multiscale, and history-dependent physical systems.

During my doctoral work with Novelis Switzerland, I developed and validated multiscale models for friction, lubrication, surface evolution, and third-body wear in aluminium sheet-metal forming. I combined Boundary Element, Discrete Element, and Finite Element approaches with experimental surface data and custom scientific software.

My earlier work in computational materials science combined finite elements, cellular automata, and neural-network surrogates to model microstructure evolution in stainless steel. I am particularly interested in advanced modelling, scientific software, and AI-assisted engineering workflows.

Computational Materials · Neural Networks

FE–Cellular Automata–Neural Network Microstructure Modelling

Developed a coupled framework for cold-rolled 304L stainless steel that linked finite-element deformation fields, cellular automata, and a neural network to predict martensite reversion, recrystallization, and evolving microstructure during non-isothermal annealing.

  • Abaqus/Explicit thermo-mechanical rolling simulation
  • Cellular automata for recrystallization and grain evolution
  • Neural network for martensite-reversion prediction
  • Validated with XRD, Feritscope, metallography, hardness and tensile data
