About

Research and engineering with a computational lens

I am a researcher and engineer working in computational mechanics and materials science, with a strong interest in connecting mathematical models, numerical simulation, and engineering decision-making.

My work spans finite element analysis, contact mechanics, multiscale modelling, microstructure-informed simulation, and scientific computing workflows applied to materials and tribological systems.
